Our story

A family kitchen that grew into a dastarkhwan

It started with one karahi pot and a small tandoor near the chowk. Today the recipes are still the same: hand-ground masalas, desi ghee, and meat that arrives fresh every morning. No shortcuts, no food colour, just the taste you remember from home.

Come with the whole family, or let us bring dinner to your door.
